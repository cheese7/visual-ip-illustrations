## Composition Families

### Workflow

Use for input-to-output reasoning, editorial handoffs, review loops, learning sequences, decision paths, repair flows, and method steps.

Draw a loose left-to-right or curved path. Tux operates the decisive handoff point: pointing to a turn, carrying a source card, sorting envelopes, bridging stepping stones, repairing a broken link, stamping a checkpoint, or routing a small signal through the path.

### System Local View

Use for filters, decision points, hidden dependencies, evidence clusters, priority shelves, comparison layers, and resilience checks.

Draw only 3-5 core parts. Tux works inside one decisive part: inspecting a lens, untangling threads, weighing claims on scales, shielding a fragile idea, tuning a lamp, repairing a lever, or connecting two shelves.

### Before/After

Use for vague-to-clear, tangled-to-readable, scattered-to-sorted, fragile-to-stable, overloaded-to-focused, and stuck-to-moving changes.

Left side is unstable. Right side is calmer. Tux performs the transformation in the center with a knot, bridge, map, gate, shelf, scale, stamp, source card, route marker, or small hand-built machine.

### Character State

Use for focused review, careful comparison, overloaded sorting, guarded judgment, stuck reasoning, and confident handoff.

Show Tux in 2-4 compact states. Each state has one short label and one physical action: inspect, point, carry, sort, bridge, repair, guide, stamp, mark, tune, shield, weigh, connect, untangle, map, compare, lift, assemble, or route.

### Concept Metaphor

Use for judgment scales, trust bridges, decision gates, tangled threads, map routes, evidence shelves, review lamps, hidden keys, routing switches, and small strange machines.

Build one memorable low-tech object or small hand-built machine. Tux is the operator of that object, with eye direction, flipper position, beak direction, seated posture, and oversized webbed feet tied to the action.

### Method Layers

Use for layered methods, capability stacks, reasoning ladders, evidence shelves, review depth, confidence building, and staged handoffs.

Draw stacked boxes, shelves, steps, or loose construction layers. Tux carries, shields, stamps, sorts, tunes, repairs, weighs, bridges, lifts, assembles, or routes one layer so the method becomes usable.

### Map Route

Use for idea paths, reader journeys, publishing routes, migration paths, decision trails, and problem-resolution routes.

Draw one curved route with a few nodes. Tux guides, bridges, reroutes, repairs, stamps, marks, compares, maps, carries, or routes the decisive path segment.

### Mini Comic

Use for failure-to-fix sequences, repeated reasoning mistakes, handoff stories, comparison lessons, and small process stories.

Use 2-3 loose panels. Each panel shows one Tux action. Keep Tux consistent, active, source-reviewed, and article-explanatory across panels.

## Supporting Object Pool

Use small, physical objects that make article logic visible:

- maps, folded routes, dotted paths, and signposts
- stepping stones, bridges, gates, shelves, and ladders
- knots, threads, ropes, hooks, clips, and loose wires
- lamps, lenses, compasses, scales, stamps, keys, buckets, envelopes, and cards
- shields, levers, pulleys, tiny cranes, simple switches, and hand-built machines
- source cards, license tags, review stamps, attribution tags, and boundary tags
- small terminals as abstract props, paper paths, split routes, and handoff boards

Keep the pool sparse. Use 3-6 supporting objects for most images.

## Linux Mascot Action Patterns

- Pointing flipper: Tux points to the useful turn, risky gap, stable claim, or next action.
- Carrying source: Tux carries a source card, key, envelope, lamp, stamp, map piece, or bridge part.
- Sorting table: Tux sorts cards, buckets, labels, paths, or evidence pieces.
- Bridge repair: Tux bridges or repairs two separated ideas, sections, or handoffs.
- Route marker: Tux maps, marks, routes, or reroutes the decisive path segment.
- Shield stance: Tux shields a fragile idea, boundary, source note, or decision.
- Scale check: Tux weighs two claims, tradeoffs, paths, or choices on scales.
- Thread untangle: Tux connects or untangles threads, trails, shelves, handoffs, or competing claims.
- Lift and assemble: Tux lifts, assembles, or tunes a small machine, shelf, bridge, route, or review ladder.

Tux action verbs stay physical and readable: inspect, point, carry, sort, bridge, repair, guide, stamp, mark, tune, shield, weigh, connect, untangle, map, compare, lift, assemble, and route.
